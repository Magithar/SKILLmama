# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Participants should assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and
accept constructive feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publishing private
information without consent are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact details on the
repository profile. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
